Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

1. Information We Collect

Feedglot is designed to work without an account and without tracking.

  • We do not collect, store, or process any personal information on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your feed subscriptions, read/starred state, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud Sync, your feeds, articles, and app settings are synchronized through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This data remains in your personal iCloud account and is subject to Apple's privacy policies.

3. Translation Services

Feedglot can translate article titles and content using:

  • Apple on-device translation (processed locally on your device).
  • Online translation engines you configure yourself, such as DeepSeek, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude. Your article text is sent directly from your device to the provider you choose, using your own API key. We do not intermediate, log, or store these requests.

4. RSS Content

Feedglot fetches RSS/Atom feeds from URLs you provide. We do not control or host this content. Please review the privacy practices of the publishers you subscribe to.

5. Data Retention

Local data is retained according to the retention setting you choose in the app. You can delete the app or clear its data at any time from your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Feedglot is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
