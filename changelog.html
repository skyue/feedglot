2026-11-03

1.2.0 · Build 110

Mac and iOS

New features

  • Added local agent tools on macOS to query feeds and articles, read article content, and manage subscriptions, categories, read status, and stars.

Improvements

  • Unified reading list sorting by when articles were first added, newest first.
  • Updated the description of Pro benefits.

Fixes

2026-10-24

1.1.2 · Build 104

Mac and iOS

New features

  • Open images in a lightbox on iOS and macOS. Zoom and pan, then return to your reading position when you close it.
  • Tap to load embedded Bilibili, YouTube, and HTML5 videos. Videos do not autoplay.

Improvements

  • Aligned menu labels, icons, order, and grouping. iOS uses fixed-position status icons, while macOS uses native checkmarks for feed Reader View settings.
  • Shortened Reader View menu labels and made them reflect whether the action enters or exits Reader View.
  • Title translation remains free and unlimited. The free full-text allowance has ended, and full-text translation requires Feedglot Pro.
  • Separated automatic translation settings for titles and full text. Automatic full-text translation requires Pro.
  • Aligned settings and purchase screens around “Unlock full-text translation” in English and Chinese.

Fixes

  • Fixed menu layout shifts when toggling Reader View and clarified the macOS menu state.
  • Fixed playback for some article videos and added a browser fallback when loading fails.

2026-10-11

1.1.1

Mac and iOS

New features

  • Restore the last reading position when reopening articles on iOS and macOS. Positions are stored on the device.
  • Set a feed to always use Reader View from the article’s More menu on both platforms. The setting is shared with the feed menu.
  • Keep iOS awake during foreground refresh and restore normal screen locking when refresh ends or the app leaves the foreground.

Improvements

  • Prioritized custom names and applied localized natural sorting to feeds and categories in subscription and bulk management lists.
  • Moved the current article’s Reader View action to More and aligned menu labels and groups across platforms.

Fixes

  • Fixed vertically stretched images in some articles.
  • Fixed list crashes caused by duplicate article IDs. Prevented new duplicates and cleaned up existing ones while preserving read and starred status.
  • Fixed a blank share sheet the first time it opened on iOS.

1.1.0

  • Fixed some articles reverting to unread during sync.
  • Improved iCloud sync stability and fixed crashes and interface issues in some cases.
  • Fixed fetching and saving RSS items without a GUID.
